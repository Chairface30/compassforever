# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Exploration only: the challenge pages and tracker are gone (the game's own
  Legacy window already shows them).
- A new window: every zone with how much is left, and the chosen zone's own
  map with the fog still over what you haven't found, a marker per area, the
  route as a line and you on it. Click a marker to go there next.
- The world map shows a bar with Start route for the zone on screen, and its
  markers can be clicked to head there next.
- Route me automatically when I enter a zone: starts the route and says which
  way to run. Each uncovered area is announced with the next stop.
- Routes are exact for up to 11 areas.

## First version

- Every Legacy challenge with its steps, a tracker for any of them, and all 43
  zone maps area by area with the shortest walking route, a waypoint arrow and
  map markers. Spots come from the game's own terrain.
